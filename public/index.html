          Bonk Brawl · netcode

          Rollback netcode, in a browser.

          The fight runs as a deterministic integer-only simulation. Your inputs apply instantly, your rival's are predicted, and when a late input disagrees the game rewinds and re-simulates the frames before you ever see them.
