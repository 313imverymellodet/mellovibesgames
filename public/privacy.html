Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Short version: our games on the App Store and Google Play have no accounts and save your progress only on your phone. They offer optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, which can use your device's advertising identifier, and a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase handled by Apple or Google. We never receive your name, email or payment details.

Information we collect

We (Mello Vibes Games) don't collect personal information directly. The apps don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and they don't send your progress to our own servers. The services below process some data on our behalf or under their own policies.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games offer optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, such as a continue or a power-up. There are no banner ads and no ads you didn't ask for. These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, advertising ID on Android) and other device identifiers;
  • general device information (model, operating system, language) and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or watched) and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads software.

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices on iPhone: the game asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before ads may use your advertising identifier. You can say no and still play with every feature, and change your answer in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Your choices on Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and an Ad Choices button in the game's menu lets you change that choice at any time.

In-app purchases

Each game offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase that grants rewards without watching a video. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The store tells the app whether the purchase was made, so it can be restored if you reinstall the game.

RevenueCat: some of our iPhone games, including Milky Space Diner, use RevenueCat to confirm and restore that purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase history in that app, and device information such as the app version and language. It also receives reports of ad revenue from AdMob, so that we can see ad and purchase income together. It doesn't receive your name or payment details.

What stays on your device

Each game saves your progress on your phone so you can pick up where you left off: best scores, unlocked items, in-game currency, the daily reward or challenge streak, whether you own Remove Ads, and settings such as sound and vibration. The game doesn't send this to us or to any service, apart from the purchase records described above, though your phone's own backups may include it. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used by Google AdMob to load ads, and by the store and RevenueCat for purchases.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, AD_ID on Android): used by Google AdMob, as described above.
  • Billing: lets Apple or Google Play process the Remove Ads purchase.
  • Notifications: some games ask after your first daily reward. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in your phone's Settings.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. Most games have a switch for it in their menu, and your phone's settings can turn off haptics everywhere.

The apps don't request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Sharing your score

When you tap a Share button, the game opens your phone's share sheet with a short text message (for example your score). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. Whatever happens next is covered by the privacy policy of the app you share to.

Children

The games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The web versions

This policy is about the App Store and Google Play apps. The browser versions on play.mellovibes.io and partner game sites have their own online features and ads, which are described on those sites.

Changes

If we change what the apps collect, we will update this page, and each app's App Privacy and Data safety answers, before that version is released.

Contact and deleting data

Questions about privacy, or requests to delete data: use the support link or developer contact shown on the game's App Store or Google Play listing. We hold no account or progress data for you, so we can pass deletion requests for purchase records on to RevenueCat.
