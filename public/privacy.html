Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Short version: our Google Play games have no accounts and save your progress only on your phone. They show optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID, and they offer a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase handled by Google Play. We never receive your name, email or payment details.

Information we collect

We (Mello Vibes Games) don't collect personal information. The apps don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and they don't send your progress or anything about you to our own servers.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games offer optional rewarded videos: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, such as a continue or a power-up. There are no banner ads and no ads you didn't ask for. These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • general device information (model, operating system, language) and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or watched) and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads software.

Google handles this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and a Privacy button on the main menu lets you change that choice at any time. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases

Each game offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase that grants rewards without watching a video. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. Google tells the app only whether the purchase was made, so it can be restored if you reinstall the game.

What stays on your device

Each game saves your progress on your phone so you can pick up where you left off: best scores, unlocked items, in-game currency, the daily challenge streak, whether you own Remove Ads, and settings such as sound and vibration. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used by Google AdMob to load ads, and by Google Play Billing for purchases.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): used by Google AdMob, as described above.
  • Billing: lets Google Play process the Remove Ads purchase.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in each game's menu.

The apps don't request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Sharing your score

When you tap a Share button, the game opens Android's share sheet with a short text message (for example your score). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. Whatever happens next is covered by the privacy policy of the app you share to.

Children

The games are intended for players aged 13 and over and are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

The web versions

This policy is about the Google Play apps. The browser versions on play.mellovibes.io and partner game sites have their own online features and ads, which are described on those sites.

Changes

If we change what the apps collect, we will update this page and each app's Data safety section on Google Play before that version is released.
