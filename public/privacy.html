Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Short version: our Google Play games don't collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in them. Your progress is saved only on your own phone.

Which apps this covers

This policy covers the Mello Vibes Games apps published on Google Play:

AppPackage
ORBYTcom.mellovibes.orbyt
Grave Shiftcom.mellovibes.graveshift
Snack Monstercom.mellovibes.snackmonster

Information we collect

None. The apps don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and they don't send anything about you or your device to us or to anyone else. They don't use advertising IDs, analytics SDKs, crash reporters or ad networks, and they work fully offline.

What stays on your device

Each game saves your progress on your phone so you can pick up where you left off: best scores, unlocked items, in-game currency, the daily challenge streak, and settings such as sound and vibration. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in each game's menu.

The apps don't request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

Sharing your score

When you tap a Share button, the game opens Android's share sheet with a short text message (for example your score). Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself. Whatever happens next is covered by the privacy policy of the app you share to.

Children

The games don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

The web versions

This policy is about the Google Play apps. The browser versions on play.mellovibes.io and partner game sites have optional online features (such as leaderboards) that work differently and are described on those sites.

Changes

If an app ever starts collecting data, for example by adding online leaderboards, we will update this page and the app's Data safety section on Google Play before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the developer contact email shown on each game's Google Play listing.
